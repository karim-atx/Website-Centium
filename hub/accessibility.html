Accessibility Statement

Our commitment

Atraxia is committed to making atraxia.org usable by as many people as possible, including people using assistive technology. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA as a practical target, though this page has not yet had a formal accessibility audit — treat this as a statement of intent rather than a certification.

What we've done so far

  • Visible keyboard focus states on interactive elements
  • Semantic HTML landmarks (nav, main, footer) and heading structure
  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful images; decorative graphics marked so screen readers skip them
  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion for people sensitive to animation
  • Color contrast checked against the page's light and dark palettes

Known limitations

This site has not yet been tested with a screen reader end-to-end or audited by a third party. If you hit a barrier, we want to know — see below.

Report an issue

Found something that doesn't work with assistive technology, or is hard to use? Email hello@atraxia.org with the page and what happened, and we'll look into it.
