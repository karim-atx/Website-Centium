# Centium Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. Confirmed by legal counsel.

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for what you buy from Centium. It's part of Centium's Terms of Service.

### 1. Who we are

Centium is operated by Atraxia. You can reach us through Help in the Centium app or at centium.atraxia.org. Prices are shown by Apple or Google before you buy, in your local currency, including the taxes your country requires.

### 2. What this policy covers

This policy covers what you buy from Centium, always inside the Centium app through Apple's App Store or Google Play:

- **Centium Premium**, the paid plan for members, at $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial;
- **professional plans**, paid monthly or yearly (the free professional plan costs nothing);
- **business plans**, paid monthly or yearly;
- **hiring credits**, which businesses use to hire professionals through Centium.

It does **not** cover what you buy from a gym, studio or professional through Centium, such as memberships, classes, sessions or products. You pay them directly, and their own refund rules apply. Centium isn't a party to those sales, though we'll help you contact them.

### 3. Your right to withdraw within 10 days

Under Lebanese consumer law, if you buy from Centium online as a consumer, you may withdraw within **10 days** of buying, **unless you have started using what you bought during those 10 days**.

Because Centium Premium and business plans start the moment you buy them, we'll ask you to confirm before you buy: *"I want my plan to start now. I understand that once I start using it, I can no longer withdraw."* If you buy and don't use it at all within 10 days, ask Apple or Google for a refund as described in section 6, and contact us too so we can support your request.

### 4. Subscriptions after you've started using them

Once you've started using a paid plan, payments aren't refundable, except where the law requires it or in the cases in section 7.

- **Free trial.** Premium can start with a 7-day free trial, once per Apple ID or Google account. Before it starts, we show the price you'll pay after the trial and the date it renews. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing; otherwise your chosen plan starts and is charged when the trial ends. We remind you in the app 2 days before it ends.
- **Cancel any time.** Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, then stops renewing. We don't refund the unused part of a monthly or yearly period.
- **Yearly plans** follow the same rule: cancelling stops the next renewal.
- **Switching plans** (for example monthly to yearly, or a bigger professional or business plan) takes effect as shown when you confirm the change.

### 5. Professional plans, business plans and hiring credits

These are bought for work, not as a consumer. A professional plan isn't refundable once you've used it. A business plan is bought before onboarding and isn't refundable once the business starts onboarding. For both, cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period. A hiring credit is used when a hire is accepted and isn't refundable once used; unused credits don't expire and can't be exchanged for money. If a professional's paid plan ends, the Premium their clients received through it ends too (section 8).

### 6. Refunds are made by Apple or Google

All Centium purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment, and their refund rules apply: request a refund from them directly, Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com and Google through Google Play. Centium can't issue refunds for App Store purchases. You cancel subscriptions in your Apple or Google account settings.

### 7. When we'll help

Whatever sections 4 and 5 say, we'll support your refund request to Apple or Google, and credit your account where we can (for example extra Premium days or a replacement hiring credit), when:

- you were charged twice for the same thing;
- you were charged after cancelling in time;
- you were charged the wrong amount;
- Centium was unavailable to you for 7 or more days in a row through our fault.

### 8. Points, perks, and Premium included by others

**Points, rewards and vouchers** are earned, not bought. They have no cash value and can't be exchanged for money or refunded. If a business wrongly refuses a valid voucher, Centium staff may return the points you spent on it.

**Premium perks** (extra points, streak shields, Premium-only offers, early class booking and waitlist priority) are part of Premium, not sold separately, and have no cash value. Offers and early booking depend on each gym or professional choosing to provide them, and may change.

**Premium included through a professional or gym** is paid for by them. It lasts while the conditions shown in the app are met (for example, actively working with a verified professional on a paid plan). It ends 7 days after they stop being met, with a notice in the app and an offer to subscribe, and there's nothing to refund because you didn't pay for it.

### 9. How to ask for help with a purchase

Refunds are requested from Apple or Google (section 6). For help, or to report a problem listed in section 7, contact us through Help in the Centium app, with your account email, what you bought, the date and the reason. We'll reply within 5 business days.

If you dispute a payment with your bank or card provider instead, we may pause the plan concerned until the dispute is resolved.

### 10. Accounts closed for breaking our terms

If we close or suspend an account because it broke our terms, we don't refund the remaining paid period, except where the law requires it. If points, perks, vouchers or Premium were obtained by breaking the rules (for example with fake accounts or fake referrals), we may remove them or stop them, without refund.

### 11. Price changes

If we change the price of a plan, we'll tell you at least 30 days before your next renewal. You can cancel before the new price applies.

### 12. The law that applies

This policy is governed by Lebanese law, and the courts of Beirut have jurisdiction. Centium is available in every country where the App Store and Google Play offer it. Nothing in this policy removes rights the law of the country where you live gives you that can't be waived; for example, where a law gives a 14-day right to withdraw from digital services, that right applies as that law sets it out.

### 13. Language

This policy is published in English and Arabic. If they differ, the Arabic version prevails.

### 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll show the date of the latest version at the top, and tell you in the app before any change that reduces your rights takes effect. Changes don't apply to purchases made before them.
